The idea

Something happens.
Something fires.

Every automation people flex — the n8n builds, the WhatsApp bots, the "AI agents" — is the same skeleton underneath: a trigger and an action, stacked.

So don't start with Python, a $2,000 course or 20 hours of tutorials. Start with one small workflow that runs on its own. This is it:

Every morning at 8, your phone gets today's high and the chance of rain. You never touch it again.

You'll need

  • Telegram on your phone.
  • An n8n account (n8n.io). Use Cloud so it keeps running when your laptop is shut. It's paid after the free trial, so check current pricing.
The real test

Build it, publish it, then don't touch it for three days. The first time it fires while you're asleep, it clicks.

Step 1 of 6
STEP 013 min

Make your bot.

  1. In Telegram, search @BotFather. Pick the one with the blue check.
  2. Send /newbot.
  3. Give it a name, then a username ending in bot (e.g. mymorning_bot).
  4. BotFather replies with your token. It looks like 7123456789:AAHk3x9…
Lost it?

Message BotFather /mybots → your bot → API Token.

Keep it private

Anyone with the token controls your bot. Blur it in every screenshot. If it leaks, hit Revoke current token on that same screen.

Your first automation03
Step 2 of 6
STEP 022 min

Get your Chat ID.

  1. Open your new bot — not BotFather — tap Start, and send it "hi". A bot can't message you until you've messaged it. Skip this and nothing works.
  2. In your browser, open this (your token goes where it says, right after the word bot): 
    https://api.telegram.org/bot<YOUR_TOKEN>/getUpdates
  3. Find "chat":{"id": and copy the number after it.
Two numbers. Don't swap them.

Token has a colon — 7123456789:AAH… — and goes in the credential.
Chat ID is just digits — 583920471 — and goes in the node's Chat ID field.

Page shows "result":[] ? Message your bot again and refresh.

Steps 3–4 of 6
STEP 03 · THE TRIGGER2 min

8 AM, every day.

  1. In n8n, create a new workflow.
  2. Add a Schedule Trigger: every Day, hour 8, minute 0.
  3. Open the workflow's Settings and set Timezone to your city. Skip it and it fires at 8 on the server's clock.
STEP 04 · THE DATA3 min

Get the forecast.

  1. Add an HTTP Request node. Method: GET.
  2. URL — this one's New York; swap in your city's latitude, longitude and timezone: 
    https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast?latitude=40.71&longitude=-74.01&daily=temperature_2m_max,precipitation_probability_max&temperature_unit=fahrenheit&timezone=America%2FNew_York&forecast_days=1
  3. Test step. You should see temperature_2m_max and precipitation_probability_max.

Open-Meteo: free, no API key, for personal non-commercial use.

Your first automation05
Step 5 of 6
STEP 05 · THE ACTION5 min

Send it to your phone.

  1. Add a Telegram node → Send a text message.
  2. Credential to connect with → Create new. Paste your token into Access Token. Look for "Connection tested successfully."
  3. Chat ID: paste your Chat ID.
  4. Text: switch the field to Expression and paste: 
    Good morning.
High {{ $json.daily.temperature_2m_max[0] }}°F · {{ $json.daily.precipitation_probability_max[0] }}% chance of rain
  5. Add Field → Append n8n Attribution → off, to drop the "sent with n8n" footer.
  6. Test step. Your phone should buzz.

Step 6 of 6
STEP 0610 sec

Publish it.
Walk away.

Click Publish, top right. Save only keeps a draft — nothing runs on schedule until you publish. Edit it later? Publish again.

Then leave it alone for three mornings. If a message doesn't land, open the Executions tab: it shows whether it ran and where it stopped.

If it breaks

It's one of these.

ErrorFix
Chat not foundSend your bot "hi", then get the Chat ID again from getUpdates. It must be your chat with your bot, not BotFather.
Bad request — check your parametersOpen the error's Details. It's almost always "chat not found" (above) or an empty message (below).
UnauthorizedWrong token, or a Chat ID pasted where the token goes. Re-copy it: /mybots → your bot → API Token.
Message is empty or says undefinedThe expression can't find the data. Run the HTTP Request step first and check the field names match.
Nothing came at 8 AMDid you hit Publish? Is the timezone set? Check Executions.
Honest note

The first two rows are the exact walls I hit building this one. Everyone hits them. They're not you.
